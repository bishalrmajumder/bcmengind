What personal data we collect and why we collect it ?

Browsing the website

You can browse this website without creating an account, filling in a form or giving us any personal details. The website has no comment section, no login and no online payment.

Enquiries and quotations

When you contact us by email, phone call or WhatsApp, for example to ask for a quotation, price list or catalogue, we receive the details you choose to share with us. These usually include your name, company name, phone number, email address, delivery location, GSTIN and the products and quantities you need.

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotations, process and deliver orders, raise invoices and provide after-sales support. We do not use it for any unrelated purpose without your consent.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set its own cookies. It stores one small item in your browser (local storage): the web address of our latest price list, so the "Price List" button opens the right document. This item contains no personal data and stays only on your device. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Embedded content and third-party services

To load quickly and display correctly, this website uses a few trusted outside services:

  • Google Fonts (Google LLC), for the website's typefaces
  • Cloudflare cdnjs, for the 3D background graphics
  • Images hosted on hindustanenterprise.net, our group website

When your browser loads these, the service receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you are viewing, as with any website you visit.

Links on this website to Google Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram, X (Twitter) and hindustanenterprise.net take you to those services. Once you are there, their own privacy policies apply, and they may collect data about you, use cookies and track your activity, especially if you are logged in to them.

Analytics and advertising

We may advertise our products through Google Ads. If you reach this website by clicking one of our ads, Google may use cookies to measure how the ad performed and to show you relevant ads later. You can control this through your Google ad settings. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.

Who we share your data with & how long we retain your data ?

We share your details only when it is necessary to serve you or required by law:

  • with transport and courier partners, to deliver your order
  • with our accountants, tax authorities and government portals such as GST and GeM, for invoicing, tax filing and tenders
  • with the manufacturers whose products we supply, such as ESAB as their authorised dealer, when needed for warranty or technical support
  • with government or law enforcement authorities, when we are legally required to

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to serve you, and in any case no longer than 3 years after our last contact with you. We keep invoices and order records for at least 8 years, as Indian tax and accounting laws require. After that, we delete the data or make it anonymous.

What rights you have over your data ?

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you
  • correct or update your details
  • delete your personal data, except data we must keep for administrative, legal, tax or security purposes
  • stop sending you product updates, price lists or offers
  • withdraw any consent you have given us

To use any of these rights, or to raise a privacy concern or complaint, email us at bcmengineeringindia@gmail.com or call (033) 4024 5170. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Where we send your data ?

Your enquiry details are handled through the email, phone and WhatsApp services we use, which may store data on servers in India or abroad. We take reasonable care to keep your information safe, and only our staff who need it to serve you can see it. However, no method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
